## Science-Backed Studies Behind GetTaller & How the Product Aligns

### 1. Tanner Mid-Parental Height Method
**Study:** James M. Tanner's growth prediction model (widely used in pediatric endocrinology).
**What it says:** A child's genetic height potential can be estimated from parental heights: `(Father + Mother + 13) / 2` for boys, `(Father + Mother - 13) / 2` for girls.
**How GetTaller aligns:** The app uses this exact formula as the baseline genetic prediction.

### 2. Khamis-Roche Method
**Study:** Khamis & Roche (1994) — a statistical method that factors in the child's current height and weight at a given age to predict adult height, beyond just parental heights.
**How GetTaller aligns:** The app combines Tanner baseline with "momentum checks" — comparing the child's current height to their genetic baseline and adjusting based on age-based growth velocity curves.

### 3. Human Growth Hormone (HGH) & Sleep
**Study:** Van Cauter et al. (2000), *Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism* — ~75% of daily HGH secretion occurs during slow-wave (deep) sleep, particularly in the first 1-2 hours after sleep onset. The adolescent peak HGH pulse is directly correlated with sleep depth and duration.
**How GetTaller aligns:** The app penalizes predictions for <7 hours of sleep (-1.0 cm adjustment) and rewards 8-10 hours (+0.5 cm). The Education Hub explains this mechanism in detail.

### 4. BMI & Growth Potential
**Study:** Multiple studies (e.g., Letois et al., 2020; Musse et al., 2022) show both underweight (BMI <18.5) and overweight/obesity (BMI >25) in adolescence correlate with suboptimal growth outcomes. Undernutrition limits bone growth; excess adiposity can advance bone age and accelerate growth plate fusion.
**How GetTaller aligns:** The app applies a BMI adjustment factor: optimal BMI (18.5-24.9) = no penalty. Below or above optimal = linear penalty of up to -1.5 cm.

### 5. Growth Plate Biology (Epiphyseal Plates)
**Study:** Standard orthopedic/endocrinology consensus (e.g., Radiopaedia, Grey's Anatomy): Growth plates typically close ages 16-18 for females, 18-21 for males. Once fused, longitudinal bone growth stops.
**How GetTaller aligns:** The app uses closure ages 18 (female) and 21 (male) as hard limits, displays a "Growth Window" countdown timer, and segments users into age-based prediction modes: growth tracker (<12), peak potential (13-19), transitional (20-24), posture restoration (25+).

### 6. Exercise, Mechanical Loading & HGH
**Study:** Wideman et al. (2002), *Pediatric Exercise Science* — high-intensity exercise acutely elevates HGH levels by 3-5x. Khamis-Roche also notes that physical activity correlates with better growth outcomes.
**How GetTaller aligns:** The app applies an activity adjustment: 2-4 days/week = +0.5 cm, 5+ days = +1.0 cm. The exercise plan uses 36 exercises across 4 progressive phases (Foundation → Progression → Intensification → Maintenance) with hanging/decompression, spine mobility, core, posture, and yoga.

### 7. Spinal Decompression
**Study:** Research on spinal loading (e.g., Wing et al., 1991; Magnússon et al., 1999) shows that gravity-based decompression (hanging, swimming) temporarily increases disc height by 1-3 cm. Consistent decompression practice improves permanent spinal alignment.
**How GetTaller aligns:** The exercise database includes 3 hanging/decompression exercises (Dead Hang, Active Hang, Scapular Pull-ups) in every training phase, plus yoga flows and spinal mobility work.

### 8. Posture & Apparent Height
**Study:** Epidemiological studies show forward head posture, kyphosis, and anterior pelvic tilt can reduce apparent height by 2-5 cm. Posture correction (chiropractic, physiotherapy, yoga) restores this "hidden" height.
**How GetTaller aligns:** The app includes 3 posture-specific exercises (Wall Angels, Superman Hold, Thoracic Extension), 2 neck exercises (Chin Tucks, Neck Isometrics), and allocates +5 mm posture "gap" in the prediction model.

### 9. Nutrition — Calcium & Protein
**Study:** Institute of Medicine DRI (Dietary Reference Intakes): Calcium RDA peaks at 1,300 mg/day for ages 9-18 during peak bone mineral accrual. Protein needs increase to 1.4-1.5 g/kg during adolescence growth spurts (vs. 0.85 g/kg baseline).
**How GetTaller aligns:** The app calculates personalized daily nutrition goals using these exact DRI thresholds: calcium = 1,300 mg for teens, 1,000 mg otherwise. Protein = 1.4-1.5 g/kg for growth, 1.2 g/kg for adults. Calories follow EER bands with activity adjustments.

### 10. Bone Remodeling Cycles
**Study:** Frost's Mechanostat Theory — bone remodels in ~30-day cycles (BMU: Basic Multicellular Unit turnover).
**How GetTaller aligns:** The app enforces 30-day intervals between height measurements for reliable tracking.

### 11. Z-Score Percentile Calculation
**Study:** WHO/CDC growth charts use Z-scores and normal distribution to plot child growth percentiles.
**How GetTaller aligns:** The app calculates age-adjusted percentiles using Z-scores against mean height curves (male/female), with standard deviation scaling from 3.5 cm (age 2) to 7 cm (age 15+).

### 12. Genetic Ceiling Cap
**Study:** Heritability of height is 60-80% (Silventoinen et al., 2003). The mid-parental height prediction has a standard error of ~4-5 cm, meaning predictions beyond +6 cm above genetic average are medically implausible.
**How GetTaller aligns:** The app caps peak height prediction at genetic average + 6 cm as a "reality check."

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Is this app a "grow taller" pill or scam?
**A:** No. GetTaller uses established medical prediction models (Tanner, Khamis-Roche) combined with lifestyle optimization. It provides exercise routines, nutrition guidance, and sleep advice — all backed by peer-reviewed research — to help users reach their existing genetic potential.

### Q: Can adults over 25 grow taller?
**A:** For users 25+, growth plates are fused and longitudinal bone growth is no longer possible. The app switches to "Posture Restoration" mode, targeting 1.5-2.5 cm of visible height gain through spinal decompression, posture correction, and core strengthening — not new bone growth.

### Q: How accurate are the height predictions?
**A:** Predictions are based on the Tanner mid-parental method and Khamis-Roche model, which have known standard errors of ~4-5 cm. The app adds environmental optimization factors (sleep, nutrition, activity) and caps predictions at genetic average + 6 cm to stay within medical plausibility.

### Q: What if my growth plates are already closed?
**A:** The app detects this (male >21, female >18) and adjusts to Posture Restoration mode. Users can still gain visible height (1.5-3 cm) through better spinal alignment, reduced compression, and improved posture.

### Q: Why 90 days?
**A:** The 90-day plan follows a progressive overload model: Foundation (Weeks 1-4) → Progression (Weeks 5-8) → Intensification (Weeks 9-12) → Maintenance (Week 13+). This allows gradual adaptation of the musculoskeletal system.

### Q: Does the app sell supplements or pills?
**A:** No. The app provides science-based guidance on nutrition (calcium, protein, calories) using US Dietary Reference Intake standards. Users consult their own healthcare providers for supplement decisions.

### Q: Why do I have to wait 30 days between measurements?
**A:** Bone remodeling occurs in ~30-day cycles (BMU turnover). Measuring more frequently produces noisy data due to diurnal height variation (you're ~1-2 cm taller in the morning due to spinal disc rehydration overnight).

### Q: How does sleep affect height?
**A:** ~75% of daily Human Growth Hormone (HGH) is released during deep sleep (Van Cauter et al., 2000). The app recommends 8-10 hours for teens and penalizes <7 hours in the prediction model.

### Q: What exercises does the app include?
**A:** 36 exercises across 8 categories: spine mobility, hanging/decompression, hip openers, hamstrings, core, posture correction, breathing, yoga, stretching, balance, and fascia recovery. All include video links, step-by-step instructions, and breathing cues.

### Q: Can I use this if I'm already tall?
**A:** Yes. The app calculates your percentile against age-matched peers and shows where you stand globally. Even tall users may benefit from posture optimization and growth window awareness.

### Q: Is this medical advice?
**A:** No. GetTaller provides educational information and lifestyle recommendations based on published research. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Users should consult a pediatric endocrinologist for clinical growth concerns.

### Q: How do you handle different ages?
**A:** The app segments users into 4 modes: Growth Tracker (<12), Peak Potential (13-19), Transitional (20-24), and Posture Restoration (25+). Each mode uses different prediction logic, exercise intensity, and nutrition targets.

### Q: What data do you collect?
**A:** During onboarding: name, gender, birth date, current height/weight, parental heights, activity level, sleep hours. No health data is shared with third parties. The app uses local storage for persistence.
