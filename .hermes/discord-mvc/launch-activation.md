## THE REVIEW TEMPLATE (give to ambassadors)

Keep it natural — no copy-paste same review. Give them a template:

```
Structure:
1. What the app does (1 sentence)
2. What you liked (1-2 sentences)
3. Who it's for (1 sentence)

Examples:
"Finally a height app that isn't selling pills or fake programs.
The 90-day plan is actually structured and the AI coach gives
solid nutrition advice. Good for teens who want a real plan."

"Been using the dead hang tracker and sleep logging for a week.
The growth chart is cool — shows you your potential based on
your parents' height. No subscription BS, just ads. Worth a try."

"Downloaded for the exercises, stayed for the meal tracking.
The AI actually breaks down your food and tells you what's
missing for growth. Works well for anyone 13-25."
```

## RISK MANAGEMENT

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Google Play flags review spike as fake | Reviews are from real users with varied wording. No copy-paste. Space them over 24-48 hours, not all in 1 hour. |
| Crash on certain devices | Ambassadors tested APK on minimum 5 different devices during early access |
| Ad fill rate low at launch | Multiple ad units + fallbacks. If banners fail, the app still works. |
| Discord server gets raided | Carl-bot verification on join. Slow mode in #general-chat. Instant ban on invite links. |
| Low turnout at launch time | Start recruiting NOW. Target 200 members — even 50 active at launch is enough for a signal. |
| Negative reviews from non-community users | They'll come eventually. Respond professionally: "Sorry you feel that way. We're actively updating — DM me on Discord if you want to help shape the fix." |
