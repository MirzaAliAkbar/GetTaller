# GetTaller — Affiliate Program Details

## Overview

GetTaller offers a revenue-share affiliate program for content creators, influencers, and health/fitness educators. Partners earn commissions by driving new app installs through unique tracking links.

---

## Commission Structure

### Revenue Share on Ad Revenue

Since GetTaller is a free app (ad-supported), affiliates earn a **percentage of the ad revenue generated by users they refer**.

| Milestone | Commission Rate | Revenue Share Duration |
|-----------|----------------|----------------------|
| **Start** | 5% of referred user ad revenue | 3 months per referred user |
| **5,000+ total installs** | 20% of referred user ad revenue | 12 months per referred user |

**How it works:**
- You start at **5% revenue share** from day one
- Once your total referred installs reach **5,000 lifetime**, your rate jumps to **20%**
- At the 5K milestone, the revenue share duration also extends from 3 months to **12 months** per referred user
- Revenue share applies to all ad formats: banners, interstitials, and rewarded ads

**Example earnings (estimated):**
- At 5% with 1,000 active users generating ~$1.50/month each = **$75/month**
- At 20% with 5,000+ active users = **$1,500+/month**

## Tracking Method

**Available now:**
- **Unique referral code:** Each affiliate gets a personal code (e.g., `SARAH20`) to share in their bio, video, or caption. Users enter it during app setup.
- **Affiliate dashboard:** Your own dashboard showing installs, revenue generated, earnings, and user retention — updated regularly.
- **Attribution:** First code wins, and it's tied to that user permanently (within the revenue-share duration).

**Coming in Phase 2:**
- **Vanity tracking links** (e.g., `gettaller.app/ref/CREATORNAME`) that auto-apply your code.
- **Deep links** to specific app sections (onboarding, education hub, exercises).
- **Postback API** for high-volume partners needing server-to-server validation.

*Phase 2 link-based tracking is on the roadmap; for now, code-sharing is how referrals are counted.*

## Creator Requirements

### Minimum Eligibility
- Active social media account with **1,000+ followers** (any platform)
- Content focused on health, fitness, self-improvement, lifestyle, or education
- Must disclose affiliate relationship per FTC guidelines (e.g., "#ad", "#affiliate")

### Content Guidelines
- Must NOT make medical claims or promise specific height results
- Must NOT target children under 13 (COPPA compliance)
- Must NOT use misleading thumbnails or clickbait about "guaranteed" height gain
- Approved messaging themes:
  - "Reach your genetic potential through science-backed habits"
  - "Optimize your growth window with proper nutrition, sleep, and exercise"
  - "Posture correction for visible height improvement"
  - "Track your growth journey with data-driven insights"

### Prohibited Content
- Claims of guaranteed height increase
- Before/after photos implying the app caused growth
- Medical advice or diagnoses
- Content targeting minors with weight/body image messaging
